Process of Work

Water-tightening treatment

What is water-tightening?

We can enlighten our customers in a very simple way, water-tightening is a process, where we thoroughly check all the points in a washroom / kitchen etc. which leads to wall dampness and seepages. Sanitary seepage is due to multiple issues and needs to be attended asap before it damages your house. We have a unique solution called water-tightening as mentioned below.

As per the Thermography scanning, the images (Normal & Thermal) will be shared along with the quotation which would be marked & indicated if there is any sanitary seepage. Pigmentation seen in purple colour shows water / moisture accumulation in those respective areas.

  1. All drain outlets are rectified and water tightening process in done in all washrooms / Kitchen /Balcony & Dry Balcony ( as per the survey feedback)
  2. Our process is done on surface area which includes 80% Mechanical and 20% Chemical process.
  3. A 4-to-5-inch slit is made at the threshold of the washroom entrance, where we place a High Speed impactor to blow out the excess moisture and chemical treatment is done there for precaution, duration of keeping the impactor is as per the moisture collected below the tiles. (Anti-skid Black or white tiles will be replaced).
  4. Checking of concealed piping in washrooms by pressure testing is done in every flat to ensure if there is no crack or pipe break, this only can be done if there is an independent valve outside each washroom .
  5. Gravity testing is done to ensure if there is no leakage in the washbasin and W/C (water closet checking also is done to see if there is any multiple sources of sanitary seepage due to which water gets accumulated in the sunk).
  6. We have marked the points in the images where the process needs to be done.

Duration: - Each washroom 1 day and next day can be used after the chemical washing is done. Total Days to complete the work 3-4 days.
